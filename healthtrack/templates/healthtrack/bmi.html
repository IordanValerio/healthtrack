What is BMI and what is it for?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple indicator, used by the World Health Organization (WHO), to classify whether a person is underweight, at a healthy weight, overweight, or obese. It is calculated through the division of your weight (kg) by your height (cm) squared: BMI = weight / (height x height). It serves as a tool to identify risks of diseases associated with weight. Results between 18.5 and 24.9 are considered normal, although it does not consider muscle mass, so always consult a doctor.
